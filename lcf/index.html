How to Play

Build your club from Division 5 to the top. Here is everything you need to know.

🎯 Your Goal

Lead your club through 38 rounds each season. Top 3 teams are promoted, bottom 3 are relegated. Win the Division 1 title and become a legend!

📅 Season Flow

Set your squad and tactics, then play the next match from the Match Center. After Round 38 the season ends, salaries are settled, and promotions or relegations are decided.

⚽ Match Modes
  • 🎮 Play Match – control a player yourself in a live 11 vs 11 match.
  • 👀 Watch Match – the AI plays for you. Choose the speed and enjoy.
  • ⏩ Sim 1 Match – instantly simulate just the next match.
  • ⏭ Sim Rest of Season – instantly simulate every remaining round.
🎮 Controls in Play Match
Stick / Arrows / WASD Move
Z Pass / switch player
X Hold to charge a shot, tap to slide tackle
Stick / Arrows right after a shot to curve the ball

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen joystick (push lightly to walk, fully to sprint) and the Z / X buttons below the pitch. A Bluetooth or USB gamepad also works: left stick to move, A to pass, X / B to shoot.

🧠 Squad & Tactics
  • Pick a formation and a tactic: Attack, Balanced or Defense.
  • Tap two players (or drag & drop) to swap starters, bench and reserves.
  • Tired or injured players perform worse. Rotate your squad!
  • Use the Training Center to boost ratings. Recovery can be used once after each match.
💰 Money & Survival
  • Income comes from matches, sponsors and prize money.
  • Player and coach salaries are paid once at the end of Round 38.
  • Sell bench players for about 70% of their value (less for veterans) and sign free agents in the FA Transfer Market.
  • Fall deep into debt and you will be sacked for bankruptcy. Finish in the bottom 3 of Division 5 and you are sacked too.
💾 Saving & Career

Your progress is saved automatically in this browser. Use Career Records to see your trophies and history, and Export / Import JSON to back up or move your save. If you get sacked, new clubs may offer you a job, so your career never ends.
