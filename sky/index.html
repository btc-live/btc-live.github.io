FIELD MANUAL
HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

  • Shoot down the target number of enemies before time runs out.
  • The sortie fails if your energy hits 0 or time expires. Energy slowly recovers when you avoid hits for a while.

PC CONTROLS

  • Move mouse — aim; the plane turns toward the crosshair
  • Left click (hold) or Space — fire machine guns
  • WASD / Arrow keys — climb, dive, bank
  • X speed up · Z slow down (or use the throttle lever on the right)
  • Esc — pause

MOBILE CONTROLS

  • Touch and drag to aim and turn; guns fire while you hold
  • Slide the throttle lever on the right up/down to change speed

HQ SCREEN

  • DIFFICULTY — 5 levels. Harder levels need more precise aim but pay more score and SP.
  • Sortie cards — pick an unlocked sortie. Earn up to 3 stars (★) per sortie.
  • OPEN HANGAR — spend SP to buy and equip aircraft, and on upgrades (shared by all aircraft).

TIPS

  • Get on the enemy's tail and aim slightly ahead of it.
  • Flying slow turns tighter but makes you an easy target. Keep your speed up.
  • Aces are very tough. Upgrade before taking them on.
